About

I'm an independent IT consultant based in Budapest, working across SAP Business Warehouse (SAP BW), SAP Analytics Cloud, and business intelligence. My background bridges business and technology: I hold a Master's in Business Information Systems (FHNW, Switzerland) and a Master's in Regional and Environmental Economics (Corvinus University of Budapest), and spent several years in analytics and operations roles before moving fully into IT consulting.

That combination — business fluency plus technical depth — is what I bring to every engagement: I don't just build the dashboard or the data model, I make sure it answers the question your business actually has.
